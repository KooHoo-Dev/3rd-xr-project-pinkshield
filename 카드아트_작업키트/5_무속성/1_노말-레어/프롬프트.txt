I'm making original trading card art for a VR game about cute little jelly monsters (slimes).
Follow these rules for every image in this chat unless I say otherwise.
The attached images are anchors: copy their art style, body shape, face, jelly material and lighting for the tier I am working on.
Priority: always cute first, a little monster second.
Each request ends with its tier (Normal, Rare, Super rare, Unique or Legendary).

1. BODY (Normal to Unique)
- One soft rounded dome-shaped jelly body like a mochi or gumdrop: about 1.2 times wider than tall, a big round top, nearly straight sides that curl slightly inward at the bottom edge, a flat bottom
- These are little monsters, not objects with faces: each one has ONE creature feature that grows from its own body (horns, a row of jelly spikes, scales, a shell, crystals, small wings, frills). It is joined to the body with no seam
- Never: ears, hair, arms, legs, a tail, a neck, humanoid features, a teardrop or onion shape where the jelly body itself is pulled up into a pointed tip on top (features such as horns, flames or crests may grow anywhere, including the center)
- Effects (fire, water, wind, rocks, lightning) wrap around the body and never reshape it; nothing below the body may look like legs or a stem
- The slime sits on the ground unless I say it floats or jumps
- Materials: the jelly body is glossy. Features made of natural materials (horn, rock, lava crust, shell, bone, ore, feathers, wood) look natural and a little weathered: matte or softly satin, fine cracks, slight wear, a little dust or moss where it fits, never plastic-shiny. Naturally shiny materials (crystals, gems, pearls, ice, metal) keep their own shine but with small natural imperfections such as rough bases, tiny inclusions or light scuffs. Features grown from the jelly itself (jelly spikes, jelly fins, jelly coral) stay glossy like the body. Keep the wear subtle and clean so it stays cute, never dirty or gross
- The jelly body and its feature belong together: the feature's story continues into the jelly (for example glowing cracks or veins running from a crust or horn down into the body, tiny embers, bubbles, crystal specks, chips or cloud wisps suspended inside), and the jelly itself has subtle depth: soft inner mottling, a gently glowing core and a soft satin sheen, never a flat plain plastic surface. Where a feature meets the jelly, the base blends in with a soft glow or a few small fragments sinking into the jelly

2. COLOR
- One main body color plus ONE accent color that I name, used on the tip of the creature feature, the inner core or small details only

3. FACE (every tier except Legendary)
- Two round glossy eyes set wide apart, a little below the middle of the body, one flat very dark color (dark reddish-brown on warm slimes, dark navy on cool slimes), no iris, no gradient, no sparkles inside
- Each eye has exactly two white highlights: a large one upper-left and a small one lower-right
- Short faint brows, a small thin-lined mouth (default: a tiny "w" smile), a soft pink blush under each eye toward the outside
- Each request may change ONE personality feature (a tiny fang, two tiny fangs, a tongue, a wide open mouth, smug or sharp eyes, a pout, a wink, a crouching pose). Everything else on the face stays like the anchor. Even angry or fierce faces stay cute: only the brow angle and mouth shape change, never scary
- Never scary or gross: no mouth full of teeth, no bloodshot or tiny pupil eyes, no tentacles, no melting or dripping slime goo

4. STYLE BY TIER
Normal, Rare: 3D toy
- 3D rendered like a glossy collectible vinyl toy, matching the anchor: smooth clean surfaces, soft lighting, a brighter inner core, a soft specular highlight top-left
- No outlines, no brush strokes, no painted or 2D illustration look
- Normal simple; Rare a few more features and small effects
Super rare: detailed illustration
- A polished, detailed fantasy card illustration matching the attached steel slime anchor (copy only its style, detail level and quality, not its color or element): clean thin outlines, painterly shading, glossy translucent jelly, fine details (layered plates, engraved seams, glowing cracks, translucent layers showing the core), a strong colored rim light from behind, and a dynamic action with bold effects
- Super rare keeps the dome body and the anchor face
- Super rare effects have three layers of depth: a trail or burst behind the body, an arc or ring around it, and debris or droplets flying toward the viewer in the foreground. Give the body one contrasting material or value (for example dark rock, steel or deep shadow against a glowing core) so the character never melts into its own effect color
Unique, Legendary: rich illustration
- A rich polished fantasy illustration matching the style anchor: glossy jelly with painterly detail, ornate decorations grown from its body or made of element materials, a glowing core and many tiny light specks inside the body. At least the fine detail and crisp finish of the attached Super rare steel slime quality reference (copy only its detail level and finish, not its color, element or design): clean thin outlines, painterly shading, fine material details on every feature (engraved or carved patterns, glowing seams and cracks, crisp facets, layered translucent jelly showing the core), and even more ornate than Super rare
- Unique keeps the dome body and the anchor face, but feels like a full-art premium trading card: a dynamic action moment, not a standing mascot. Seen from a slightly low three-quarter angle so it looks powerful, the body leaning toward the viewer while keeping its soft dome shape, its creature feature thrust forward with slight foreshortening. It is actively using its power: a big burst of its element erupts from it and sweeps around the body in a curved arc that frames it, never covering the face, with crisp clean edges. Many four-pointed star sparkles and bright light dots are scattered around it. Vivid saturated colors with strong contrast, crisp cel-shaded fantasy illustration with sharp highlights and clean shadow shapes, a bright core lighting the body from inside. The expression shows the personality of its title and may combine two personality features
- Legendary: the same rich illustration style at an epic, mythic scale, like a giant ancient monster. The body may grow beyond the dome shape, but keep at least two of: glossy jelly, an inner glow, a slime face. The eyes may be mythic (glowing, slit pupils, no mouth, no blush). A glow behind the body is allowed
- Legendary also follows the Unique full-art action rules, pushed to a mythic scale: an even lower camera angle looking up at a giant, its power erupting across most of the frame, a large glow behind the body. Its creature feature is part of its own body, never a separate object or background scenery.
- Every tier: semi-translucent mostly opaque jelly, key light from the top-left, a clear bright rim light from the back-right outlining the whole silhouette, the body color slightly deeper toward the bottom

5. IMAGE
- One character centered, full body, square 1:1. The character fills about 65% of the frame width with a small even margin (Super rare, Unique and Legendary: the character and its effects may fill most of the frame, keeping a small margin)
- Plain solid flat background in the color I name, no gradient, no floor, no scenery, no contact shadow
- Effects have crisp clean edges, no smoke, mist or glow haze on the background (Legendary glow is the only exception)
- No text, no letters, no numbers, no border, no frame, no stars, rarity symbols, icons, badges, logos, signatures, watermarks or stickers anywhere in the image (especially not in the corners)

EGG images ("Egg:"): an object with no face, same style and image rules.
BACKGROUND images ("Background:"): soft painterly 2D painting, gentle gradients, low detail, slightly blurred, light from the top-left, mid-tone values, no creatures, no text.
FIELD images ("Field:"): a clear detailed painterly landscape as the main subject, light from the top-left, a calmer simpler lower third, no creatures nearby, no text.

Avoid: anything that looks like an existing game, anime or mascot character (if a design starts to look like one, change its shape and colors), teardrop-shaped slimes, ears, limbs, everyday objects worn as costumes (hats, ribbons, helmets, propellers), anything scary or gross.
