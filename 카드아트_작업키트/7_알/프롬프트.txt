============================================================
[세션 설정]
============================================================
I'm making background and scenery art for trading cards in a VR game about cute jelly slime monsters.
Follow these rules for every image in this chat unless I say otherwise.

COMFORT (every image)
- Mid-to-dark tones overall. No large white or very bright areas: the cards are viewed in VR and big bright areas tire the eyes
- Light comes from the top-left
- No text, no letters, no numbers, no border, no card frame, no stars, rarity symbols, icons, badges, logos, signatures, watermarks or stickers anywhere in the image (especially not in the corners)

BACKGROUND (a request starting with "Background:")
- A soft painterly 2D digital painting, gentle gradients, low-to-medium detail, slightly blurred with a shallow depth of field, like a stage backdrop
- A slime character will be placed on top later, in the center. Keep the center calm and open: empty ground or floor in the lower middle, left bare for the character to be added later (do not draw it), nothing standing, sitting or floating in the center, no strong lines or objects crossing it
- A soft glow behind the center, where the character will be, is the brightest part of the image
- Interesting shapes stay at the left and right edges and far in the distance
- No characters, no creatures, no animals, no slimes, no faces, no people, no human figures, no silhouettes, not even tiny ones in the distance

FIELD (a request starting with "Field:")
- A clear, detailed painterly landscape that is the main subject of the card, in focus, not blurred
- No creatures nearby. Tiny distant slimes only when I ask for them

EGG (a request starting with "Egg:")
- A soft painterly scene matching the attached egg image: the egg is the main subject in the center, in focus, with its surroundings
- The egg is an object: no face, no eyes, no mouth

LAYOUT
- "Landscape 4:3": the main subject or open space is in the center
- "Portrait 2:3": the top tenth and the bottom third are calm, simple and darker because card text covers them; the main subject or open space sits just above the center
