# Slime Card Art Brief

*A Day as a Card Shop Part-Timer* (VR, Meta Quest) — 50 trading cards

Thank you for drawing these! This brief lists every card with a short concept, plus the few rules that keep the 50 cards looking like one set. Your own style is welcome — the rules below are only about shape, layout and file format.

---

## 1. How the cards are seen in the game

- The player holds each card in their hand in VR. A card is a real trading-card size: **63 × 88 mm** (2.5 × 3.5 in).
- At that size, **bold shapes and clear colors** read better than fine detail. Simple is good, especially for Normal cards.
- The game draws the card frame, the card name, the rarity label and the price text. **Please don't draw any text, letters, frame or border** in the art.
- The slime and the background are shown on two separate layers, so the slime looks like it floats slightly in front of the background when the card tilts (a 3D effect). See section 5.

---

## 2. The slimes — core rules

These are the only must-follow rules. Everything else is up to you.

### Body
- One soft, round **dome-shaped jelly body** — like a gumdrop or a mochi. A little wider than tall, round top, gently flat bottom.
- Small variations are fine: flatter or puffier, a slight squish or tilt, a bottom edge that spreads out a little like a puddle.
- **No arms, legs, ears, hair, tail or neck.**
- Things that sit on or stick to the body are fine: crystals, leaves, a hat or helmet, small wings, a bow, a hairpin.
- Effects (fire, water, wind, rocks) go **around** the body. They never change the body's shape, and nothing hanging below the body should look like legs.

### Face (all slimes except Legendary)
- Two round, dark, glossy eyes, set wide apart, a little below the middle of the body.
- One or two small white highlights in each eye.
- A small thin mouth (default: a tiny "w" smile).
- A soft pink blush under each eye.
- No eyelashes — **except the Coral Princess (W-U) and the Deep Sea Queen (W-L)**, who may have a few short lashes.
- Expressions change only the eye and mouth shape (sleepy, happy closed eyes, determined, surprised, shy, grumpy).
- **Legendary** slimes may have mythic eyes instead (glowing, slit pupils, no mouth, no blush).

### Please avoid
- A pointed, teardrop-shaped slime (looks like a famous existing game character).
- **Crowns** on slimes (same reason). Royal or noble slimes wear headpieces made from their element instead: flames, shells and pearls, coral, leaves, feathers.
- Anything from existing shows or games, including *Avatar: The Last Airbender*. The four elements are a nod to it, but please no symbols, characters, clothing, hairstyles or creatures from the show.

## 3. Rarity — how fancy each card is

Every element has 8 slime cards. The higher the rarity, the more special the slime looks. The body shape stays the same; the difference comes from decorations, effects, pose and background.

| Rarity | Cards per element | What makes it look that way | Size of the slime in the picture |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | 3 | Plain and cute. Calm pose. At most one small detail | Small, about half the picture height |
| Rare | 2 | **One** clear feature (a horn, a hat, wings, a hairpin) and a few sparkles | A bit bigger |
| Super Rare | 1 | **Power bursting out**: a glowing core, an element effect swirling around the body, an action pose, a strong expression. No armor or headpieces | Big, with the effect filling the picture |
| Unique | 1 | **Dignity**: a headpiece and ornaments made of its element, a glowing core, a calm noble face | Large, on a full-card picture |
| Legendary | 1 | **Mythic scale**: a giant slime, a huge effect behind it like a halo, mythic eyes (optional) | Huge, and it may break out of the frame |

## 4. Elements and colors

| Element | Colors | Jelly feel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fire | orange, red, warm yellow, sunset pink | warm and glowing. **Cozy at Normal, bold and fierce from Rare up** |
| Water | aqua, teal, deep blue, coral pink accents | clear, with tiny bubbles. **Water slimes have a feminine, graceful feel** |
| Earth | warm brown, sand beige, amber, steel silver, gem colors | earthy, with mud, stones, crystals and metal. **Gems and caves** |
| Air | pale sky blue, white, soft lavender | glossy, with soft white cloud swirls inside. **Things that fly in nature** |
| Neutral | **pink tones, or beige / cream / pearl (near colorless)** | classic smooth jelly |

For Air slimes, keep the body a smooth jelly dome in pale blue or lavender. A fluffy cotton-ball body loses the slime shape.

## 5. Canvas sizes and layout

There are two card layouts. The layout depends on the rarity.

### Layout A — Window card (Normal, Rare, Super Rare)

![Window card layout](images/brief_layout_window.png)

- The art sits in a window in the upper half of the card.
- **Canvas: 2048 × 1536 px (4:3).**
- Keep the slime inside the **center square** (1536 × 1536), with a little space around it.
- About 4% of each edge may be cut off. Keep important things away from the edges.

### Layout B — Full-art card (Unique, Legendary)

![Full-art card layout](images/brief_layout_full.png)

- The art fills the whole card.
- **Canvas: 2100 × 3000 px (about 2:3).**
- The **top 10%** (card name) and the **bottom 35%** (description and price text) are covered by text. The game darkens these areas a little. Keep the slime's face and the important parts in the middle band, a little above the center.
- **Legendary**: the slime may stick out over the frame. Draw it big and don't cut it off at the canvas edge.

### Layers (please)

The picture is one drawing, but please export it as **two files**:

1. **Slime layer** — only the slime and its effects, on a transparent background. PNG.
2. **Background layer** — the full background. PNG or JPG.

- Paint the background **all the way behind the slime** too. When the card tilts, the slime shifts a few pixels and the hidden area peeks out.
- Shadows on the ground: either leave them off or put them on the background layer.
- Field cards are **one file** only (no separate layer).
- Egg cards work like slime cards: the egg on one layer, the background on another.

### File names

Use the card ID with `_` instead of `-`:

```text
F_N1_char.png   (slime layer)
F_N1_bg.png     (background layer)
FD_SR_bg.png    (field card, one file)
SN_R_char.png   SN_R_bg.png   (egg card)
```

Egg cards keep the `SN` ID from an earlier version of the game.

### Backgrounds

You can paint the backgrounds yourself or use existing ones. The game will be shown publicly in a job portfolio, so **if you use someone else's background, please check that its license allows that use** and keep a note of where it came from.

## 9. Checklist for each card

- [ ] Round dome body, no arms, legs, ears or tail
- [ ] Face matches the face rules (lashes only on W-U and W-L; Legendary may use mythic eyes)
- [ ] No crown, no pointed teardrop shape, nothing from existing shows or games
- [ ] No text, letters, frame or border in the art
- [ ] Right canvas size for the card's layout (4:3 or 2:3)
- [ ] Slime inside the safe area (center square, or the middle band for full art)
- [ ] Two files: slime/egg layer (transparent PNG) + background, painted fully behind it
- [ ] File names follow the `F_N1_char.png` / `F_N1_bg.png` pattern
